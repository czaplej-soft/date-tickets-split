How to tell them apart

Use the tenant and the channel. Do not use the subject or the intake tag.

  • Parser: a day-first tenant, any way in except a booking-v3 form. The day and month trade places.
  • Hint: a form report from a tenant on booking-v3. The form prints MM/DD/YYYY and then reads the date the tenant’s way. The report reads like a parser report, so its tenant’s form decides.

Subjects do not separate them: the same subject lines appear under both the parser and the hint. Intake tags do not either, because intake guesses from the reporter’s browser.

The desk files by tenant and channel, so the parser count includes the five reports in desk-notes.json. Their bodies show a one-day move, not a swap. One meeting appears on Tuesday in Lisbon and Monday in Toronto, and one invite arrives before its booking. Five reports do not prove another cause, so they stay in the parser ticket, marked there.

What to do

  1. Keep the queued parser fix, and close only its ticket with it. Then check whether those five reports stop.
  2. Change the booking-v3 hint, or the way that form reads its field. The parser fix does not touch this.

What this does not settle

The #4471 page and decision 0001 stay as written. An accepted decision is replaced by a new record, and merging on subject alone is worth one: it put these problems in one ticket. That record belongs to its owners.
